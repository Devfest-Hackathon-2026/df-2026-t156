# Mining audio sources

All clips are CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Original clips below were renamed without altering the recording.

- `rock-hit.ogg` and `rock-break.ogg`: rubberduck, `bfh1_rock_hit_01.ogg` and `bfh1_rock_breaking_02.ogg`. https://opengameart.org/content/75-cc0-breaking-falling-hit-sfx
- `weak-hit.wav`: Brian MacIntosh (BMacZero), `clink2.wav`. https://opengameart.org/content/metal-impact-sounds
- `footstep-gravel.ogg` and `footstep-dirt.ogg`: TinyWorlds, `gravel.ogg` and `mud02.ogg`, edited from pdsounds.org recordings. https://opengameart.org/content/different-steps-on-wood-stone-leaves-gravel-and-mud
- `deposit.ogg`: Kenney, `handleCoins2.ogg` from RPG Audio. https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio and https://opengameart.org/content/50-rpg-sound-effects

Local derivatives (2026-11-08), retaining CC0: `forge-stroke.wav` is the first 115ms of BMacZero's `clink2.wav`; `bag-settle.wav` (190–350ms), `bag-tug.wav` (35–235ms), and `footstep-gravel-short.wav` (0–270ms) are excerpts of TinyWorlds' `gravel.ogg`. Converted to mono 22050Hz PCM, short edge fades and peak gain applied. These reuse the recordings above; no new external source or synthesized alarm was added.

`surface-crack.wav` is a 380ms excerpt of rubberduck's `bfh1_rock_breaking_02.ogg`, converted to mono 22050Hz PCM with an edge fade and 0.65 peak. It marks a thin gold surface breaking; the full recording remains the deeper core fracture.
